# Phase 6B — branches and stock logistics

Status: implemented; local validation is recorded in [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md). This extends Phase 6A without supplier payments, provider refunds or implicit stock conversion.

Branches use current verified Owner/Admin authorization and privileged MFA. Owner/all-branch Admin can create and set status; scoped Admin can rename/address assigned branches. Codes are immutable. At most 100 active branches are supported; at least one remains active. Deactivation refuses nonzero stock, open shifts, unfinished purchase orders, outstanding transfer quantities and every retained unreleased/unabandoned offline grant. Acquire the branch row FOR NO KEY UPDATE before the organization feed counter; metadata updates never change key columns. Current membership must reload after create/status changes.

Transfers require source/destination in the same organization, distinct branches and current authority at both endpoints for new dispatch. Dispatch removes physically confirmed goods from the source only. Destination-only staff may confirm partial physical receipts; source-only staff may confirm goods physically returned from transit. Immutable dispatch/receipt/return documents rebuild remaining quantities. Receipt versus transit return uses one expected transfer revision and serialized state. No cancellation silently restores stock. Reads permit an authorized endpoint, with foreign-endpoint balances/revisions omitted.

Supplier returns reference one original purchase receipt and its items. Whole-unit cumulative returns cannot exceed its recorded quantities or available on-hand stock. Original cost/currency/unit remain evidence; no money movement, supplier refund, email or provider settlement is claimed. Inactive product/supplier records do not erase physical-history authority; current product revision and the original unit must match. New dispatch requires active products.

All mutations bind immutable organization/actor/request UUIDs, persist encrypted local intent and attempts before RPC, and preserve uncertainty through restart and later denial. Current verified role/raw branch assignment/MFA precede replay; immutable successful actions replay before current active/CAS/settings checks. Journal branch locators may be historical and are not authorization. Money is bounded to exact JSON integers; quantities are whole units 1..1,000,000, with at most 100 unique lines. Server timestamps are UTC.

Existing stock movements, projections and transaction-held feed counter remain the only stock source. Branch share locks precede the counter; request/state locks precede sorted product locks. Source depletion never introduces/deepens a deficit. Confirmed positive receipts may reduce a trusted offline deficit. Product units freeze after ledger/order references or while a retained offline grant contains that product. Direct service branch/catalog mutations are revoked; validated definer RPCs remain available.

Required checks include scope/MFA/direct mutation denial, immutable replay and changed UUID payload, partial receive/return caps, supplier receipt provenance, exact arithmetic, atomic rollback, stock/feed effects, competing stock writers and lifecycle changes, retained grants and paid evidence, encrypted restart/retry, stale account/dialog responses, bounded paging and 320px layouts. Local checks do not prove live deployment or physical hardware.

Public branch RPCs: `get_branch_management_context`, `list_managed_branches`, `save_branch`. Logistics RPCs: `list_stock_transfers`, `get_stock_transfer`, `list_stock_transfer_events`, `dispatch_stock_transfer`, `complete_stock_transfer`, `get_purchase_receipt_return_context`, `return_purchase_receipt`, `list_purchase_returns`. Reads are bounded and scoped through definer RPCs; direct logistics-table access is revoked. New branch/stock requests are blocked until pending logistics requests are resolved. Physical evidence does not grant online or offline authority.
